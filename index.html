Privacy Policy for LingoTuli - Language Learning

Last Updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the LingoTuli - Language Learning mobile application (Package Name: com.itamadersomajinc.lingora), developed and published by IT Amader Somaj Inc. ("we", "our", or "us").

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how our application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our language learning services.

1. Information We Collect

LingoTuli is designed to respect your personal privacy. We do not require you to create an account with passwords or share sensitive personal documents to use the core features of the app.

  • Local Learning Progress: Information about completed lessons, test scores, vocabulary mastered, daily streaks, XP points, and virtual gems are stored locally on your device using encrypted/secure local storage.
  • Device & Interaction Information: Non-personally identifiable technical information (such as operating system version, device model, language preferences, and crash reports) may be processed by platform SDKs to optimize performance.
  • Microphone / Audio Data: If you use voice recognition or speech practice features, microphone input is processed in real time for pronunciation evaluation. We do not store or transmit your voice recordings to any external server.

2. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs

To provide high-quality language education for free, our application integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and device data to deliver personalized or contextual advertisements.

Third-party service providers utilized in the app:

  • Google AdMob / Google Play Services: Used to display banner, interstitial, app open, and rewarded ads.
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
    Ad Policies: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Flutter & Local Notifications: Handles local daily study reminder notifications without sending personal data to external servers.

3. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Maintaining and tracking your language learning progress and daily study targets.
  • Providing audio pronunciation playback and interactive language quizzes.
  • Delivering advertisements via Google AdMob in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
  • Ensuring application stability, diagnosing app crashes, and improving user experience.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any third-party advertisers or data brokers. Data shared with Google AdMob is strictly governed by Google's Privacy Policy for ad serving and fraud prevention.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

LingoTuli is intended for general audiences interested in language acquisition (such as high school/university students and adults preparing for international exams like JLPT, IELTS, Goethe, TOPIK, HSK, and DELF). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

6. Data Retention and User Rights (Data Deletion)

Since your study progress is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data at all times:

  • You can erase all your progress and stored data at any time by clearing the application storage via Android Settings (Settings > Apps > LingoTuli > Storage > Clear Storage/Data) or by uninstalling the application.

7. Security

We value your trust in using our language learning app. We employ commercially acceptable security standards to protect your local data and ensure safe network interactions with official APIs and Google services.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
